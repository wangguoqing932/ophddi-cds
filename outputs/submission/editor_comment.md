# Editor comment for the Frontiers review forum

**Thread title:** Revised manuscript and point-by-point responses (R1 and R2)

**Comment:**

Dear Dr. Huang,

Thank you for handling our manuscript and for giving us the opportunity to revise it. We are grateful to the reviewers for their careful reading and constructive comments.

We have revised the manuscript and prepared a point-by-point response to all 10 comments from Reviewer 1 and all 7 comments from Reviewer 2. The main changes include: a frozen-label primary analysis with the corrected labels as a sensitivity analysis; a reconciled absorption-tier evidence table; an explicit `unassessed` status for pairs without a rule or defined matrix cell; route-restricted analyses; a full re-analysis of the 30 DDInter disagreements, including an independent review by a non-author ophthalmologist; a route-aware comparison with RA-DDInter and Lexicomp; a workflow figure; and a re-audited reference list.

We have also tried to state the limits of the evidence plainly. The independent review is reported in full, including the 16 cases in which it did not reproduce our classification; the manuscript now presents the author classification as descriptive rather than validated, and retains the low high-risk sensitivity as the principal limitation.

The clean manuscript, the marked-changes manuscript, the revised Supplementary Material (clean and marked), and both response letters are being uploaded with this response. Changes are marked in red in the marked versions.

We would be grateful for any further comments.

With respectful regards,
Xianglong Yi, on behalf of all authors
