## For R1 (insert before "## Closing")

## Additional issues identified during the revision

Beyond the points raised by the reviewer, re-auditing the data and the reference list identified six issues that we corrected in the same revision:

1. **The 20 B-only cases are not a development-free subset.** The internal development log shows that B02 and B12 were corrected after engine review, B18 and B19 informed matrix design, and other B cases informed rule-gap diagnosis. The manuscript now says that neither half of the 40-case set is development-free and reports the B-only result as descriptive; the 18/20 frozen and 20/20 corrected figures are unchanged.
2. **The registry-scale denominator is 1,933 graded-severity pairs, not all matched pairs.** The DDInter export contains 4,546 registry-matched pairs; 2,613 carry Level `Unknown` and were excluded. Methods and Limitations now state this, and the 77.7% result is described as applying to pairs with a graded severity.
3. **The 80.3% figure is a composition statistic, not a causal decomposition.** Of 1,502 downgrades, 1,206 involved low-tier ophthalmic agents; 698 were assigned low by G19 and 72 by absorption-forced matrix scaling, while 436 were decided by a rule or matrix cell whose level does not depend on the tier. The text now says "involved" and labels the figure as a composition statistic.
4. **The five unsupported downgrades are the clearest unsupported subset, not the whole clinically critical set.** They comprise three DDInter-high and two DDInter-medium pairs. Twelve disagreements are on the unassessed default path (10 route-consistent, 2 unsupported), adding four DDInter-high pairs (D3-026, D3-029, D3-033, D3-080) whose low grade is not evidence of safety.
5. **The timolol evidence line was overstated.** The cited studies support approximately 78% bioavailability relative to intravenous timolol in one crossover study, not a 50–78% range or a comparison with oral doses; Table S5b now states this.
6. **Reference metadata were corrected.** Reference [45] is the 2nd edition (Routledge), and missing titles, volume or page numbers in [13–16,29] were supplied. Every DOI was checked against Zotero and Crossref; 45/45 resolve and match the cited record.
